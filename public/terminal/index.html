ALESSANDRO°TERMINAL

A command-driven, multi-window market workspace. Type in the command line, use the dock at the bottom (☰ FUNCTIONS has every function), or the taskbar up top.

WINDOWS
drag titleMove a window; drag the corner grip to resize
double-clickMaximise / restore a window (title bar)
TILEArrange every open window in a grid
CLOSEClose all windows · UNDO brings them back
COMMAND LINE
NVDAOpen a security window
NVDA GPTicker + function in one line
NovartisAnything else searches the provider's symbol master (name or ticker, all exchanges); listings outside the Twelve Data plan open through CNBC
FUNCTIONS
GPPrice chart & security overview: candles, line or area; 1D…MAX; volume, SMA, Bollinger, RSI; log scale; compare up to 3 symbols in %; your notes on the chart
wheel · dragOn a chart: zoom with the wheel, pan by dragging, double-click to reset; the legend follows the cursor
SRCHSymbol search: find any listed instrument, open it, add it to the watchlist
WLWatchlist — add/remove symbols (saved on this device)
MKTMarkets: equities, FX, crypto, metals (real quotes)
MAPHeat maps: S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Dow 30, sectors, countries, crypto, FX — coloured by 1D / 1W / 1M / 3M / 6M / YTD / 1Y change
IDXWorld indices (81): US, volatility and MSCI from Cboe (15-min delayed), Europe and its STOXX 600 sectors from STOXX, SMI / SPI / IBEX 35 from SIX, Nikkei 225, SSE Composite and CSI 300 from the Shanghai Stock Exchange, S&P/ASX 200 from ASX, Ibovespa from B3, Nasdaq Composite from FRED; FTSE 100, CAC 40, FTSE MIB, Hang Seng, KOSPI, Nifty 50, TSX, DXY and 21 more from CNBC; 1M, YTD, 52 weeks and a chart
IDX DAXWorld indices with one index on the chart
YLDGovernment yields. MARKET: intraday quotes of 141 maturities in 33 countries (CNBC quote service), changes vs past closes, 5-year daily history. OFFICIAL: curves of 35 countries and the euro area from central banks and debt offices (monthly OECD average where no daily source exists). Both: WORLD table with changes, 2s10s and spreads; GRID of every country × maturity (level or change over 1D…1Y); each country's curve vs past dates
CURVE US IT DEYield curves of up to 6 countries on one chart, or one country at several dates
CALEconomic calendar: world high-impact events (Forex Factory) with actual figures (Nasdaq) and market reaction, plus official US schedules
PFPortfolio: your transactions, positions, value and P&L in EUR or another currency (starts empty)
EXCHWorld markets: each session on a 24-hour line in your time, open / closed, time to open / close, the main index (TABLE: every exchange)
NNews: FT, Bloomberg, WSJ, MarketWatch, CNBC, The Economist, Il Sole 24 Ore, Reuters, central banks; SEC filings for the watchlist
BRFBriefing: AI summary of the real data above, with sources
MOVMovers: most active, gainers and losers among every U.S.-listed stock (Nasdaq); most shorted (FINRA short interest, % of shares, days to cover); or your instruments
SCREquity screener: every U.S.-listed stock (Nasdaq, NYSE, NYSE American) by sector, size, move, country, price and volume
COMPCompare instruments, rebased or % change, in their own currencies or converted to USD, EUR, CHF, GBP or JPY
CORRCorrelation matrix of daily returns
MENUAll functions by category (also the ☰ FUNCTIONS button, or press m)
EQEquities: S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and Dow 30 members with weights and changes
FX · CRYPTOCurrency board with the ICE dollar index (DXY), and crypto board
CMDTY · FCRVCommodities: 26 front-month futures (CNBC) with returns, EIA spot energy, gold; futures curves by contract month for 12 commodities
CB · SPRDCentral banks compared: policy rates of 39 banks (BIS), last move, 12-month change, next decision from each bank's calendar, what the market prices; history since 2006; same-day official detail. SPRD: the spread between any two of 141 market yields (BTP–Bund and other presets) with history, percentile and z-score; official 2s10s
BONDWorld government bonds (US, Germany, UK, Japan, Canada, Switzerland, euro area) with changes and spreads; US inflation and credit (FRED)
COTFutures positioning: CFTC Commitments of Traders for the major contracts
ETFETFs by asset class (140): equities, dividends, industries and themes, Treasuries, credit, commodities, real estate, international, currencies, volatility, crypto, leveraged
ERNEarnings calendar with surprise and price reaction

OMON AAPLOptions (Alpaca indicative feed): chain, volatility smile, term structure, surface, strategy payoff with expected move and max pain

SYS · TOURSystem status; guided tour
