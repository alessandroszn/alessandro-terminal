ALESSANDRO°TERMINAL

A command-driven, multi-window market workspace. Type in the command line, use the dock at the bottom (☰ FUNCTIONS has every function), or the taskbar up top.

QUANT
MC NVDAMonte Carlo: thousands of paths built from the past daily returns (block bootstrap, bootstrap or normal) — percentile fan, probability of a gain, of a loss or of reaching a price, VaR and ES at the horizon; numbered runs, reproducible
VAR NVDAValue at risk and expected shortfall: historical, normal, Cornish–Fisher, EWMA; 1 or 10 days, at three confidence levels, on a position amount; backtest with Kupiec's test and the Basel zones
VOL NVDAVolatility: close-to-close, Parkinson, Garman–Klass, Rogers–Satchell, Yang–Zhang over 21 / 63 / 252 days; EWMA; GARCH(1,1) fitted with its forecasts; volatility cone
DIST NVDADistribution of the daily returns: histogram against the normal, skewness, kurtosis, Jarque–Bera, tails, autocorrelation and Ljung–Box
BETA NVDA SPYRegression on a benchmark: beta, alpha, R², tracking error, information ratio, up / down capture, rolling beta
WIFWhat if I had bought… (WIF): purchases of stocks, ETFs or bond ETFs on real history — once or every month, with a fee — valued every day at CNBC's closes in EUR: value today, gain, your return a year, where the gain came from, the same money in a benchmark; save them
YAS IT0005…Bond yield (YAS IT0005…): coupon and maturity from OpenFIGI's ticker (or typed), your price: yield to maturity gross and net of Italian tax, accrued interest, dirty price, durations, convexity, DV01, cash flows, price if yields move
PLABPortfolio lab (PLAB): build portfolios with target weights (ETFs by ticker or ISIN, stocks, cash; or import your portfolio), save them, backtest on real history in EUR with an initial amount, monthly contributions, rebalancing and trade costs against a benchmark; risk, correlations and past crises; compare up to 4; project to a goal (Monte Carlo)
FACT SWDAFactor exposure (FACT SWDA, FACT NVDA, FACT PORT): market, size, value, profitability, investment and momentum betas on the Fama/French factors (Kenneth R. French Data Library), monthly over 5 or 10 years — alpha, R², each factor's share of risk and of the return, CAPM / FF3 / FF5 / FF5 + momentum, rolling betas; one instrument or your portfolio
EF NVDA MSFT SPYPortfolio optimiser (2–8 symbols): efficient frontier, minimum variance, maximum Sharpe, risk parity, equal weights — long only, on past returns
SEAS NVDASeasonality: returns by month and year from monthly closes, by day of the week from daily closes
